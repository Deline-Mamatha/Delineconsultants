Testing & Commissioning

Verified before
it's handed over.

IT&C Overview

IT&C is a series of activities from design, construction and operation ensuring all design parameters are met. It verifies function against design intent, OEM manuals and the owner's operational requirements, and ensures interfaces between systems are systematically integrated.

Key Activities

  • Checklist preparation as per OEM / suppliers
  • Testing and pre‑commissioning activities
  • Report sign‑off
  • Training to FM team through OEM
  • Work method statement approval
  • EM commissioning team involvement for critical equipment
  • Test as per SOP guidelines

FAT / SAT / IST

FAT — PAC · VAV · UPS & ISO transformer · LV panels

SAT — Pre‑commissioning (installation check), commissioning (functional & performance check)

IST — Integration system verification (C&E and BMS), training & demonstration support with FM & O
