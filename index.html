About Us

Deline is a design Led build organization Providing Services across India operating through Bangalore and Hyderabad studios. We design and build Laboratories, Data Centers and Office spaces, bringing Architecture, Engineering and Execution together under one roof so every project moves from concept to handover with a single point of accountability.

At Deline, we aim to redefine spaces through innovative, sustainable solutions that integrate seamlessly with technology. Our vision focuses on creating environments that enhance productivity and well-being, while remaining environmentally responsible and adaptive to future needs in the built environment.
